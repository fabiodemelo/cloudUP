Getting started

  1. Download CloudUp

    Grab the free DMG above (macOS, Apple Silicon). Drag CloudUp into Applications. Not yet notarized by Apple — on first launch, right-click the app and choose Open.

  2. Connect your first cloud

    Pick a provider, paste your endpoint and credentials, and click Test. Credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain.

  3. Mount it

    Click Mount — the connection appears in Finder's sidebar under Locations (the first time, click Enable in Finder). Open files, edit them, and changes save straight back to the cloud.
